# N-CHEF | Cuisine Head guide

Revision: 20 October 2026 | Former Chef role

Existing Chef accounts are now called Cuisine Head. Credentials, outlet assignments and cuisine assignments remain intact. Cuisine remains the section name.

## Requests

Sign-in opens Dashboard. Choose My Requests to submit ingredient quantities for an assigned cuisine and outlet. Executive Chef reviews and consolidates them. Purchaser or Admin approves them and arranges purchasing or distribution. Approval alone does not mean goods have arrived.

## Confirm deliveries

Open Cuisine Deliveries after stock is issued. Confirm the actual quantity received. For a problem choose Less quantity, Not available, Quality issues or Other and enter the affected quantity. Other requires a message. A shortage's affected quantity must match issued quantity minus received quantity.

Reporting a problem notifies Executive Chef, Purchaser and Admin. Purchaser investigates and records replacement, confirmed shortage, usable return or confirmed wastage. Receipt confirmation does not deduct stock again. Replacement deliveries require their own receipt confirmation.

You can access deliveries for your assigned cuisines/outlets and, when linked to a personal request, your own deliveries. Select the N-CHEF logo to return to Dashboard.
