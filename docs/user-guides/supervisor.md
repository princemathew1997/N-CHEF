# N-CHEF | Executive Chef guide

Revision: 20 October 2026 | Former Supervisor responsibilities

Existing Supervisor accounts now sign in as Executive Chef, with their previous credentials and assigned outlets. Purchaser (Supervisor) is a separate role created by Admin.

## Daily workflow

Sign-in opens Dashboard. Choose Review Cuisine Requests to inspect requests from Cuisine Heads. Review or revise quantities and notes, then consolidate requests for the next level. Purchaser or Admin approves them or prepares vendor orders. Approval and stock distribution are separate actions.

Use Available Stock for assigned outlets and Stock History to understand stock movement. Cuisine Deliveries shows delivery status and problems. Notifications reports cuisine receipt problems; coordinate with Purchaser, who owns resolution and stock corrections.

Executive Chef cannot approve purchases, receive or distribute stock, change catalog/setup data, or create accounts. Only Admin manages accounts and assignments.

Select the N-CHEF logo to return to Dashboard. Shared item choices refresh every three seconds while the app is visible, with entered form text preserved.
